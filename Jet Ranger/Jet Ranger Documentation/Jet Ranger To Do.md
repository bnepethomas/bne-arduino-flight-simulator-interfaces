- [ ] **Map the three new ADF switches in FSUIPC for the Dodo.** Joystick 4 buttons 17, 18 and 19 (indexes 112, 113 and 114) go to the Dodo events `ANTI_ICE_SWITCH_SET`, `HYDRAULICS_SWITCH_SET` and `CAUTION_LIGHT_SWITCH_SET`, with 1 on press and 0 on release. Use either this mapping or the bridge's `ANTI_ICE_SET` command for anti-ice, not both. The Dodo may ignore the plain `ANTI_ICE_SET` event.
- [ ] **Navigation radios (NAV1 and NAV2).** The Radio controller's NAV displays are local only: the knobs and the swap switches change the display, and `NAV1_STANDBY:`, `NAV2_STANDBY:` and `NAV1_RADIO_SWAP` / `NAV2_RADIO_SWAP` go to the simulator, but **no bridge sends the simulator's NAV frequencies back** and the sketch has no tokens for them (it handles `C1A`, `C1S`, `C2A` and `C2S` only), so the NAV display can drift from the simulator and starts at 109.000 / 111.000 and 110.000 / 112.000 whatever the aircraft has. The MSFS bridge already reads all four NAV frequencies (they show in its readout) but does not send them; the FSUIPC bridge does not read them. To do: add NAV tokens (for example `N1A`, `N1S`, `N2A`, `N2S`) to the bridges and the sketch, apply the 500 ms encoder hold-off as for COM, and for DCS and X-Plane add radios generally (neither bridge sends COM or NAV frequencies or handles the radio commands on port 27001; X-Plane has the `sim/cockpit2/radios/actuators` frequency datarefs, DCS-BIOS has per-aircraft radio controls). The sketch part needs the Radio flashed.
- [ ] **Decide whether the Radio displays need 3 digits after the decimal point.** The COM and NAV displays show frequencies as `xxx.xxx` (the sketch pads every frequency to 3 decimals with `padFrequency()`, so the two columns line up and COM2 / NAV2 are not pulled left behind the faceplate). COM runs in 25 kHz steps (118.000, .025, .050, .075), which needs 3 digits to show the last digit; NAV runs in 50 kHz steps, which needs only 2 (the last digit is always 0); and FSUIPC holds COM to 10 kHz, so the simulator may show 118.025 as 118.02 or 118.03. Check what the real radios and the panel layout call for, and whether the NAV displays (and the DME) should drop the third digit.
- [ ] **Simulator-dependent keystrokes from the non-ADF (Upper) keypad.** The Upper numeric keypad (J32 on the Upper ADF Controller PCB) should send different keystrokes depending on the simulator being flown, mainly for radio interactions: **DCS F1 to F10**, **MSFS 1 to 10** (keys 0 to 9 of the keypad). **`*` should toggle the ATC windows** and **`#` should pause** the simulator. Today the keypad types plain keys through the USB HID board (`Upper key pressed: <key>`), which does not know the simulator. To decide: where the mapping lives (the bridge for the running simulator sending the keystrokes on the PC, or telling the HID board which table to use), the key for each of the 12 keys per simulator (and for Prepar3D / FSX and X-Plane), and the exact keys that toggle ATC and pause in each.

The panel emulators show the lamps if a bridge sends `LDGN`, `LDGL` and `LDGR`.
